# Cake Chase — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Cake Chase does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal information from anyone, including children.

- No account or sign-in is required.
- Your best score, statistics and settings are saved only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the game or use "Reset progress" in Settings.
- The game has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party tracking or SDKs that collect data.
- The game does not use the internet and does not request any device permissions.
- Because no data is collected, there is no data to share with third parties.

## Children

Cake Chase is suitable for all ages, including children under 13. It does not ask for or collect any personal information, and it contains no ads, links out of the game, chat or social features.
